The Typer

Write a message once. Tappy types it into whatever window you are in, then waits and does it again.

  • Presses Enter after each message, or leave that off
  • Repeat delay as short as 0.1 seconds
  • Live countdown to the next message

The Clicker

Clicks wherever your mouse is sitting. Park the cursor on the target and let it run.

  • Left, right or middle button
  • Single or double click
  • As fast as one click every 0.01 seconds

How it works

Three steps, then hands off

  1. Set it up

    Pick Typer or Clicker. Write your message or choose a click speed and button.

  2. Press your hotkey

    Hit F6, or change it with the gear in the top corner of the app.

  3. Switch windows

    You get a 3 second countdown to click into the right window before anything happens.

Before you download

Two things to know

Windows may warn you

Tappy fakes keyboard and mouse input, so some antivirus tools and Windows SmartScreen flag it by mistake. If you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.

Check the rules of your game

Many online games ban auto clickers and auto typers. Use Tappy at your own risk, and only where it is allowed.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need Python or anything else installed?

No. Tappy is a single app. Download it, unzip it if it comes zipped, and open it.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The download is for Windows only.

Does the hotkey work when Tappy is in the background?

Yes. The hotkey works from any window, so you can start and stop without switching back to Tappy.

Will it remember my hotkey?

Yes. The hotkey you pick in the settings is saved and loaded the next time you open the app.

Why does my antivirus complain?

Programs that send keystrokes and clicks look similar to some malware, so automatic scanners sometimes guess wrong. It only types and clicks. Nothing else runs.
